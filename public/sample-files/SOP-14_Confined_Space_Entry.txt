SOP-14 · CONFINED SPACE ENTRY
Revision F · Issued by HSE Department
Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited

Section 3. Authorization

3.2  Entry into any vessel, tank or pit classified as a confined space requires
a signed Confined Space Entry Permit, countersigned by the Shift Engineer and
the Area Safety Officer. The permit is valid for a single shift only.

3.3  A standby attendant shall remain at the entry point for the full duration
of the work and shall not leave the point unattended.

Section 4. Atmospheric Monitoring

4.1  Continuous gas testing for O2, LEL and H2S is mandatory for the duration
of entry, logged at 15 minute intervals on the entry permit.

4.2  Entry shall be suspended immediately if oxygen falls below 19.5 percent
or LEL exceeds 10 percent, and the space re-tested before re-entry.
