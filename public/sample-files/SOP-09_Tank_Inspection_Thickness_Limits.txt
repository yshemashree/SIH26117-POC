Revision C · Issued by Inspection and Reliability Engineering
Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited

Section 4. Shell Course Retirement Criteria

4.1  Scope
This section applies to atmospheric and low pressure storage tanks under the
scheduled external inspection program, including tanks in the crude and
product storage terminal.

4.2  Minimum retirement thickness
For shell courses of this tank class, minimum retirement thickness is 11.0 mm.
Any course reading below nominal by more than 1.5 mm shall trigger an
engineering review before the next inspection cycle.

4.3  Engineering review trigger
Where a shell course reading falls below the minimum retirement thickness in
clause 4.2, the inspecting engineer shall raise an engineering review request
within 5 working days and shall not close the inspection record until the
review is logged.

4.4  Re-verification
Localized findings such as pitting near weld seams shall be scheduled for
ultrasonic re-verification within 30 days of the finding being logged.
